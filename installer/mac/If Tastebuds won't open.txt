Tastebuds - first time opening it

Tastebuds is a free hobby app and isn't signed with a paid Apple certificate,
so the first time you open it, your Mac asks you to confirm. You only do this
once.

1. Drag Tastebuds onto the Applications folder in this window.
2. Open your Applications folder and double-click Tastebuds.
3. A message says Apple could not verify Tastebuds. Click Done.
4. Open System Settings, then Privacy & Security.
5. Scroll down. Next to "Tastebuds was blocked", click Open Anyway.
6. Enter your Mac password if asked, then click Open.

From now on, Tastebuds opens like any other app.

On macOS 14 (Sonoma) or older, there is a shortcut instead of steps 3 to 6:
right-click Tastebuds in Applications, choose Open, then click Open again.

If the Mac says the app "is damaged and can't be opened": open the Terminal
app, paste the line below, press Return, and open Tastebuds again.

    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Tastebuds.app
